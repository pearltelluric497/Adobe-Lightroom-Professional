## 🔍 Managing Your Workflow

Success in photography requires a good routine. Start by importing images into your catalog. Use the Import button at the bottom left of your screen. Select your local folder or memory card to bring photos into the software.

Once you import your images, use the Library module to sort them. Flag your top pictures to filter out the ones you do not need. Switch to the Develop module to start your creative process. Use the sliders on the right side to change your exposure, contrast, and saturation. Your changes update in real-time on your screen.

If you find a look you love, save it as a preset. You can apply this preset to future photos for a consistent style.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting Common Issues

If you experience problems, follow these tips to find a solution.

- Software does not start: Check if your antivirus prevents the app from opening. Add an exception for the software folder.
- Slow performance: Ensure your graphics drivers are up to date. You can find these updates on the website of your graphics card manufacturer.
- Catalog database error: Close the app and restart it. If problems persist, check if your hard drive has enough space for temporary cache files.
- Missing files: If you see an exclamation mark on a thumbnail, the software cannot locate the original file. Right-click the thumbnail and choose "Find missing folder" to reconnect the link.
